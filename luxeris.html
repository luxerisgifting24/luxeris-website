About LUXERIS

For over two decades, we have been a cornerstone of the corporate gifting industry, operating from the heart of Mumbai’s trade hub at Bhuleshwar Lane No. 3. Since our inception 22 years ago, we have dedicated ourselves to building trust, curating premium quality products, and helping businesses strengthen their relationships through the art of gifting.

While our reputation was built on years of personal service and expertise in our Bhuleshwar store, we are excited to embark on a new chapter. By launching our business online, we are bringing our extensive industry experience and carefully selected collection directly to you.
