Gifting by budget

Share your budget. We suggest suitable options.

Corporate gifting requirements vary widely. A company may need practical bulk gifts for a large employee group or a more premium executive gift for selected clients and senior management. Instead of showing a single fixed package, we can suggest options based on your actual requirement.

  • Quantity: approximate number of recipients
  • Occasion: joining, festival, event, appreciation or recognition
  • Budget: approximate budget per person or overall budget range
  • Branding: whether logo customisation is required
  • Delivery: Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane or another city

With these details, we can narrow the options to products and gift combinations that are more relevant to your brief.

What is the minimum quantity for corporate gifts?

Minimum quantities depend on the product and the type of customisation required. Share your approximate quantity and we can suggest suitable options.

Depending on the product, branding may be possible through suitable methods such as printing, engraving or embroidery.

Can you create customised gift sets?

Yes. Products such as bottles, notebooks, pens, keychains and other accessories can be combined depending on the requirement.

Do you work with smaller teams as well?

You can share your quantity and budget requirement. Available products and pricing can vary depending on order size and customisation.

Do you deliver outside Mumbai?

Yes. LUXERIS serves Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane and can arrange pan-India dispatch depending on the order requirement.

How do I request a quotation?
