About Luxeris

Built on trust and experience.

LUXERIS is a Mumbai-based corporate gifting company supplying customised corporate gifts, executive gift sets, festive hampers, trophies and mementos, calendars, mugs, metal keychains, umbrellas, T-shirts and polos, and branded merchandise for businesses.

We serve companies, HR and procurement teams, event organisers and businesses across Mumbai, Navi Mumbai and Thane, with pan-India delivery. Our gifting approach combines practical product selection with company-logo branding, engraving, embroidery, customised packaging and curated gift sets for employee, client, dealer, conference and festive requirements. From employee joining kits and recognition awards to Diwali gifting and branded event merchandise, we support bulk corporate orders from selection through branding and dispatch. With 22+ years in the gifting industry, LUXERIS provides clear quotations, branding coordination, mockups before production and coordinated fulfilment for businesses that need professional gifts delivered efficiently.
