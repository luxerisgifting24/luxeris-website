About Luxeris

Built on trust and experience.

With over 22 years in the gifting industry at Bhuleshwar Lane No. 3, Mumbai, we have built strong expertise in premium gifting, customised products, and trusted client relationships.

We specialise in corporate gifting, event gifting, wedding hampers, return gifts, branded merchandise, executive gift sets, mugs, apparel, trophies, desk accessories, and personalised solutions. Quality, presentation, and timely delivery stay at the centre of every order.

After years of serving clients offline, we started Luxeris online so premium and customised gifting is easier to reach across Mumbai and beyond. Every gift represents appreciation, relationships, and brand value. We work to leave a lasting impression.
