Bulk orders

What to share when requesting corporate T-shirts or polos.

To suggest suitable apparel options and prepare a quotation, share as much of the following information as possible:

  • Quantity: approximate number of pieces required
  • Garment type: T-shirt, polo or both
  • Purpose: event, uniform, promotion or employee use
  • Branding: logo, artwork and preferred placement
  • Size requirements: size breakup, if available
  • Budget: approximate budget per piece or total budget
  • Delivery location: Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Thane or another city

Pricing and lead time depend on the garment, quantity, branding method and artwork requirements. For events with fixed dates, it is useful to share the required delivery timeline early.
