Quick answer

What works for an employee Diwali gift under ₹500?

For a ₹500 target, start with the recipient and quantity, then choose a compact festive gift that can carry your company branding without making the packaging or customisation unnecessarily expensive. Sweets and dry fruits are straightforward festive choices; mugs, keychains and other compact branded merchandise can also work when the brief calls for a non-food gift. The final per-person price should always be confirmed against the actual quantity, branding, packaging and delivery requirements.

How to stay within a ₹500 employee-gifting target

Think in total delivered cost

For corporate orders, the product is only one part of the calculation. Before approving a design, confirm the quantity, branding method, packaging, delivery destination and deadline. These variables can materially change the final per-person quotation.
